# 3. Mercenary Club as Meta-Progression

[← Index](README.md)

Eslabong wraps its combat in a full club-management meta-layer. This
file exists to document that layer honestly, because
[05. Synthesis](05-synthesis-amazingclash.md) rejects most of its
*structure* (owning and fielding a five-fighter roster) while keeping
the *idea* it serves (a persistent, seasonal reason to keep playing the
same account over time).

## The club, per the Mercenary Market Guide and Strategy Guide

- **Roster of five active fighters** per match, drawn from a larger
  owned pool, with reserves covering injuries and matchup changes.
- **Dynamic market**: rotating recruits and gear, with recruit cost in
  gold and, for stronger options, renown. Community reports (cited by
  the wiki, not developer-confirmed) describe rival-club trades as a
  parallel acquisition path, with a noted post-launch fix around
  rival-team offer behavior.
- **Club facilities**: Scouting Office (search for a specific class),
  Barracks (experience sharing), Academy (gives inactive fighters a
  share of combat XP), Medical Bay (injury recovery), Time Chamber
  (train or respec a mercenary), Training Grounds (test tactics without
  risking league points).
- **Injuries and a Form/Stamina system** that can temporarily bench a
  fighter, making reserve depth a real cost of running a thin roster,
  not just a hypothetical one.
- **Season structure**: leagues, cups (standard, plus "Chaos Cup"
  free-for-all and "Allstar" formats per the Steam FAQ), and the
  asynchronous Challenge Tower (see
  [01](01-arena-combat-and-club-management-loop.md) and
  [05](05-synthesis-amazingclash.md) for why that mode specifically does
  not transfer to this project).
- **Behavior settings** per fighter (posture, teamwork, target
  preference, commitment) that shape how the AI-controlled portion of
  the roster fights, independent of skill kit.

## Why this file matters even though the structure is being rejected

The human owner asked for a project that follows "a mesma linha" as
`amazing-dungeons` and `amazing-nauts`: grounded in real, studied
precedent rather than invented from a one-line pitch. Eslabong's club
layer is the concrete proof that "arena combat plus a persistent
meta-game gives players a reason to return season after season" is a
validated design, currently earning a 95%-positive review score at 652
reviews. What this project needs from that proof is the *retention
mechanism* (seasons, ladders, a market/economy players reinvest in), not
the specific shape (a human managing five NPCs). See
[05. Synthesis](05-synthesis-amazingclash.md) for the resolution.
