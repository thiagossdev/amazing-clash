# Eslabong Research

Deep-research reference on Eslabong (shirowita, Steam Early Access,
released 24 Aug 2026), the game the human owner named directly as this
project's primary inspiration, plus Battlerite (Stunlock Studios) as a
supporting precedent for the live-PvP half of the brief Eslabong itself
does not ship. Gathered as the first of two inspiration pillars for
AmazingClash: a real-time, one-fighter-per-player PvP arena combat game
with Path-of-Exile-2-level character build depth. The second pillar,
that build-depth reference, lives in the sibling folder
`docs/research/poe2-build-depth-inspiration/`.

Cross-references to that folder appear throughout; read
[5. Synthesis](05-synthesis-amazingclash.md) for where the two pillars
combine into this project's proposed design direction.

## Confidence Notes

Eslabong is a small, current Early Access title from a solo developer;
its own Steam devlogs and an SEO-oriented but self-aware community wiki
(`eslabong-game.wiki`, explicit that its content is "based on observed
gameplay coverage and community reports") are the best sources
available, no independent developer interview beyond the Steam posts
was located, and values/systems are expected to keep changing during
Early Access. Battlerite's coverage is stronger: a genuine
developer-authored design postmortem (Peter Ilves, Game Director) and a
directly-fetched critical review. See [Sources](sources.md) for exactly
which claims rest on which tier of source, and for one fetch that failed
outright (a Battlerite interview on redbull.com) and is not used.
