## Fetched and cited directly

- [Path of Exile 2's Director admits, "We had a lot of arrogance about how long we thought it would take", PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/path-of-exile-2/interview-gamescom-2026-jonathan-rogers):
  primary source, a direct Gamescom 2026 interview with Jonathan Rogers,
  Path of Exile 2's Game Director. Design-process honesty about
  underestimating scope, the multi-pass road to a "satisfactory"
  endgame, and the iterative redefinition of what a Unique item is even
  meant to be. Cited for design-process humility, not for a specific
  mechanic.
- [After 13 years, Path of Exile's devs are finally ready to ditch one of its most iconic and frustrating mechanics, PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/after-13-years-path-of-exiles-devs-are-finally-ready-to-ditch-one-of-its-most-iconic-and-frustrating-mechanics/):
  press piece built on direct, attributed quotes from GGG staff
  (designer "Octavian," game director Mark Roberts) in a group
  interview with PC Gamer. Primary citation for the socket-color
  friction problem in Path of Exile 1, and the developers' own
  reasoning for simplifying skill-gem socketing in Path of Exile 2 and,
  later, in Path of Exile 1 itself.
- [Path of Exile 2 Passive Skill Tree Guide, dving.net](https://dving.net/guides/path-of-exile-2-guides/passive-tree-and-weapon-specialisation):
  community guide, canonical-tier mechanical reference (same tier this
  project's sibling research gave Fandom wikis). Primary citation for
  the Travel/Minor/Notable/Keystone node taxonomy, class-specific
  starting positions on a tree shared by every class, and the Weapon
  Specialisation dual-tree system.

## Notes on source quality and fetch limitations

Path of Exile 2 is a major, heavily-covered live-service game with
extensive official developer communication (dev manifestos, reveal
streams, forum posts from Grinding Gear Games staff); this research
pass fetched two press pieces built on direct developer quotes and one
community mechanical guide, which is enough to ground this project's
build-depth *principles* (shared tree, dual specialization, the
accessibility-vs-depth tension in itemization) but is not a complete
survey of PoE2's own design documentation. A follow-up pass reading
GGG's own manifesto posts directly (not just press coverage of them)
would strengthen this folder further if build depth becomes this
project's active development focus rather than an early blueprint
reference. No paywalled or JS-heavy fetch failures were encountered for
this folder specifically; all three direct fetches succeeded on the
local extractor tier.
